## Product name

Enhanced Snap

## Short description

Windows Snap, enhanced: the same snap gestures, layouts and snap groups, with rounded corners and even spacing.

## Description

Enhanced Snap replaces Windows Snap on Windows 11. Every gesture you know still works — drag a window to an edge or a
corner, hover the maximize button, press Win + Z, pick a window in Snap Assist — and your snapped windows keep their
rounded corners, with even Fluent spacing between them.

While Enhanced Snap runs, it switches Windows Snap off and does the job itself. Pause it, quit it or uninstall it, and
Windows Snap is back.

EVERYTHING WINDOWS SNAP DOES
• Drag a window to a side, a corner or the top; drop it on the snap bar at the top of the screen
• Snap layouts on the maximize button and Win + Z, with snap suggestions for your recent apps
• Snap Assist with live thumbnails, by mouse or keyboard
• Snap groups in taskbar previews, Alt + Tab and Task View
• Dividers between grouped windows; Win + arrow keys across zones and monitors

WHAT IT ADDS
• Snapped windows keep their rounded corners, with even spacing scaled for each display
• A frame behind each group in Mica, Mica Alt, Acrylic or a solid color, following light and dark mode
• Drag any window over a group's empty space to fill it
• X on a group closes all its windows; a group comes first in Alt + Tab when you're in it
• Dividers stop at each app's minimum size, so no window gets squeezed
• 11 layouts per monitor, plus your own columns and rows; Shift shows the zones, Ctrl spans them
• Easy on the battery: no timers while idle, efficiency mode, solid colors in Energy saver
