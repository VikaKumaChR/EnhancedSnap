# Privacy policy

**Enhanced Snap** · effective 2 November 2026 (version 1.0)

Enhanced Snap is a window-management utility that runs entirely on your PC. It does not collect, store, transmit,
sell or share personal information.

## What Enhanced Snap does on your PC

- **No network access.** Enhanced Snap makes no network connections: no telemetry, analytics, advertising, accounts
  or crash reporting.
- **Windows and pointer.** To snap windows it follows window moves and the pointer position while you drag a window,
  and reads window positions, sizes, titles and app names to arrange them, group them and label snap groups.
- **Keyboard shortcuts.** It watches for its shortcuts — Win + arrow keys, Win + Z, Alt + Tab, Win + Tab, and Shift or
  Ctrl while you drag a window — as they happen. Keystrokes are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
- **Window pictures.** To show snap groups in taskbar previews, Alt + Tab, Task View and Snap Assist, it captures
  pictures of the grouped windows in memory and hands them to Windows for display. They are never saved to disk or
  sent anywhere, and are released when no longer needed.
- **Windows Snap setting.** While it runs with *Replace Windows Snap* on, it turns off the *Snap windows* setting in
  Windows and turns it back on when Enhanced Snap pauses, quits or is uninstalled.

## Files on your PC

- Settings: `settings.json`, and diagnostic logs (when Enhanced Snap starts and stops, and errors; they can include
  the names of apps involved), in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\EnhancedSnap`. The Microsoft Store version keeps them in its own
  package folder, which Windows deletes when you uninstall the app.
- These files never leave your PC. You can delete them at any time (*Settings → About → Open settings folder*).

## Children

Enhanced Snap collects no data from anyone, including children.
